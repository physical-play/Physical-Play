# Physical-Play

A Windows AI body-control human interface software that lets you joyfully play more and stay healthier!

**Physical-Play** turns everyday physical movements—your limbs, head, body, abdominal contractions, and tongue-throat exercises—captured through a standard USB camera into real-time mouse and keyboard control. Designed by an indie developer to help users and potential patients preserve functional mobility, sharpen spatial brain navigation, strengthen core and pelvic floor muscles, and improve sleep quality through engaging interactive play.

## Core Health & Exercise Pillars

### 1. Brain Navigation & Alzheimer's Disease Prevention (*Alzheimer Details*)
- Recent [UCSF exercise research](https://www.ucsf.edu/news/2026/02/431526/scientists-find-mechanism-how-exercise-protects-brain) and [Harvard research](https://www.medpagetoday.com/neurology/alzheimersdisease/113419) ([additional coverage](https://www.euronews.com/health/2024/12/18/scientists-believe-people-who-work-these-two-jobs-have-a-lower-risk-of-alzheimers)) highlight that frequently performing spatial route planning and hippocampus navigation—as taxi drivers do—helps preserve cognitive function and lower the risk of Alzheimer's disease, even more than many desk-bound analytical professions.
- A [study in *Nature*](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-026-02279-z) ([summary](https://organicconsumers.org/scientists-discover-a-hidden-brain-cleaning-effect-triggered-by-movement/)) also shows that movement and abdominal contractions help keep the brain clean by clearing metabolic waste.
- **How Physical-Play applies it:** Jog indoors in front of your screen while navigating familiar or unfamiliar cities in **Google Maps Street View** (avoiding outdoor fall or frostbite hazards) or exploring large virtual worlds in **World of Warcraft** (where friends can jog together in multiplayer, free to play under level 20 across beginner regions such as Stormwind City, Ironforge, Darnassus, and Elwynn Forest via portals and subways). Pick a destination in mind, explore landmarks, and practice reaching it by memory without relying on a map.

### 2. Belly Squeeze & Pelvic Floor Exercise (*Belly Squeeze Details*)
- **Belly Squeeze** uses AI pose and abdominal movement detection (requiring a capable CPU such as an Intel Core i7 for responsive tracking) to train abdominal contraction alongside **Pelvic Floor (PC / Kegel) muscles**.
- Engaging these core muscles supports brain waste clearance, belly fat reduction, core stability, better posture, bladder and bowel control, postpartum recovery and pelvic organ support for women, and pelvic circulation / prostatic wellness for men.
- **Form reminder:** Focus on tightening your core muscles rather than passively shaking joints, helping protect ligaments and bones from injury.

### 3. Tongue-Throat Exercise (*Tongue-Throat Exercise Details*)
- Physical-Play includes a guided two-stage open-mouth **Tongue-Throat Exercise** calibration:
  1. Open your mouth wide and push your tongue forward and firmly up against the palate.
  2. Keep your mouth open and lower your tongue.
- Regular practice strengthens tongue, throat, and jaw muscles to **reduce snoring during sleep**, help relieve **Sleep Apnea Syndrome**, improve sleep quality, and support overall immune health.

## Installation

1. Download **`Physical-Play-setup.msi`** from the [GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/physical-play/Physical-Play/releases).
2. Run `Physical-Play-setup.msi` and follow the setup wizard.
3. Launch **Physical-Play** from the Windows Start Menu or Desktop shortcut.

---

## Environment & System Requirements

1. **Operating System & Hardware:** Windows 11 (or Windows 10 64-bit), Intel Core i7 CPU (recommended, especially for Belly Squeeze AI detection), a standard USB camera, and a **47"+ LCD/TV** display for comfortable full-body viewing.
2. **Display Resolution:** Fixed to **Full HD 1080p (`1920x1080`)** is optimal. Lower resolutions reduce user experience, while higher resolutions (such as 4K) substantially increase required AI computing power.
3. **World of Warcraft Setup:** Place the in-game **General Chat** tab at the **bottom-left corner** of the screen so Physical-Play's OCR can reliably read coordinates from that corner.

---

## Usage

1. Click the installed **Physical-Play** app in the Start Menu or on the Desktop. It opens the main control window along with the real-time window displaying your tracked body and limbs.
2. Stand in front of your USB camera at Full HD 1080p display settings, choose your target application (**Google Maps**, **World of Warcraft**, or **YouTube**), complete pose or tongue-throat calibration when prompted, and start playing and exercising!
